About

I'm a data scientist and researcher focused on how data can illuminate the questions that actually matter — economic trends, labor markets, AI progress, and how cities work. My day job is at LinkedIn, where I work on data science across the platform. I also hold an affiliate researcher position at MIT FutureTech, where I study AI progress and its economic implications.

Before that, I was a Senior Analyst at Accenture Research, working on generative AI's labor market impact and technology forecasting. Earlier, I spent time as a Research Associate at the Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia, supporting work in macroeconomics, labor economics, and monetary policy.

I studied Joint Math-Economics at UC San Diego (B.S., 2019), where I contributed to research on WWII-era unemployment dynamics and income-education gradients in developing nations.

Outside of my main work, I write NYCuriosity — a Substack newsletter applying data-driven analysis to NYC politics and policy. Topics range from transit funding and street design to Community Board votes and parking reform. I co-founded Menti, a nonprofit providing resources to teenagers and young adults navigating education and career decisions.
