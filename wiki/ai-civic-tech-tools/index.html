AI Civic Tech Tools

Guides, skills, and tools for using AI in civic tech, including Claude Skills, MCP servers, learning frameworks, and demos others have built for civic analytics and open data.

Guides & Skills
The Cyborg Speedrun
by Andrew Rose
A three-tier guide to building fluency with Claude Code, from setting up a personal site through orchestrating agents and building custom tools.
Civic Analytics Skills for Claude
by Santi Garcese
Claude Skill for evidence-based city policy analysis using open government data from Boston, San Francisco, Seattle, and Washington DC.
Tools & Apps
Civic AI Tools
by Nathan Storey
AI-powered tools and demos for working with NYC Open Data more easily and effectively. A personal project from an engineer at NYC OTI.
Maximum New York Data Hub
by Daniel Golliher
Data visualization hub exploring new ways to see and understand NYC government.
NYPD CompStat Ledger
by Josh Greenman, Vital City NYC
Organizes and visualizes NYPD CompStat crime data in real time, layering AI-generated insights on top of the city's official precinct numbers.
Vital City Experiments
by Vital City NYC
Vital City's portal of AI-powered apps and experiments exploring NYC government and civic data.
Hearing Hearings
by Sean Eke, Citizens Budget Commission
Summarizes NYC City Council hearings using YouTube transcripts and Legistar information, making it easier to track what gets said and decided in council.
State Capacity Ecosystem Map
by Henry Grunzweig
Interactive map of roughly 225 organizations working on state capacity, including think tanks, fellowships, and government innovation labs.
