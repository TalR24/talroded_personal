Questions

Good to know

How long does a project take?

A memo is often about 1–2 weeks; data analysis and dashboards typically 2–5 weeks; full reports 4–8 weeks, depending on scope and data readiness. Rush timelines can sometimes be accommodated for an added fee.

Can you keep it confidential or sign an NDA?

Yes. By default I may reference finished, non-confidential work as portfolio, but fully confidential engagements are available and I'm happy to sign a reasonable NDA.

What won't you take on?

Advocacy or lobbying content, anything with a predetermined conclusion, and any engagement that conflicts with my Community Board seat or with NYCuriosity's coverage. When in doubt, I'll tell you up front.

Who owns the final work?

You own the final deliverable. Unless we agree otherwise, I retain the right to reference non-confidential work as portfolio, always in an analytical, nonpartisan framing.
